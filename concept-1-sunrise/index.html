A day with Dovet

Support looks different for everyone. Here's what one ordinary Tuesday can look like.

7:30am

A slow, easy morning

Help with showering, dressing and breakfast, done the way you like it, with someone you know.

10:00am

Out in the neighbourhood

A walk to the park, a stop at the library, catching up with friends. Community access, at your pace.

12:30pm

Lunch, made by you

Building kitchen confidence one recipe at a time, with a worker beside you, not in your way.

2:00pm

Working toward a goal

Study, a first job, budgeting or catching the bus solo. We break big goals into doable steps.

5:30pm

An evening by the water

Because a good life includes sunsets, ice cream and nothing on the schedule.

Support for everyday life

We're a registered NDIS provider. We'll work with your plan, your support coordinator and your family to put together the right mix.

Opportunity. Belonging.

  • Morning and evening routines, showering, dressing, meals and medication prompts. Handled with dignity by workers you get to know.

  • Getting to the gym, footy, art class, church or the shops. Doing more of what you love, with people you like.

  • Shared or individual homes with the right level of support around the clock, so home feels like home.

  • Cooking, budgeting, travel training and job readiness. Practical skills that grow your independence.

  • A break for families and carers, and a change of scene for you. Planned stays or short notice when life happens.

  • Getting to therapy, the GP, work or a weekend away, with someone who'll stay as long as you need.

Getting started is simple

  1. Have a chat

    Call or send a message. We'll listen to what you need and answer every question, with no pressure.

  2. Meet your team

    We match you with support workers based on your interests, personality and needs, and you meet them first.

  3. Start, then adjust

    Support begins when you're ready. We check in regularly and change things whenever you want.
