Three homepage directions for Dovet

All three use the Warm & Human brand board: the dove mark, the five-colour palette, the script accents and the five values. They differ in personality, layout and how they move. Open each one and scroll all the way through, because most of the motion is tied to scrolling.

Helping you rise.

  1. Concept 1Sunrise

    Calm, spacious and reassuring. The page tells one gentle story, from the dove assembling at dawn to a "day with Dovet" timeline.

    Feel
    Quiet confidence, most like the brand board
    Motion
    The logo builds itself feather by feather, a sun rises as you scroll, text lights up word by word, and the day story scrolls sideways
    Best for
    Families and older participants who want calm and clarity
    Open Sunrise

  2. Concept 2Together

    Warm and photo-led. Real people fill the dove's feathers, and visitors pick the path that fits them: themselves, a loved one, their clients or a job.

    Feel
    Friendly, social and welcoming
    Motion

    Best for
    Reaching participants, families, coordinators and new staff at once
    Open Together

  3. Concept 3Rise

    Bold and uplifting. The page moves from dawn to day to dusk, with a photo as the rising sun and big confident type.

    Feel
    Energetic and modern, stands out from other providers
    Motion
    The photo sun rises over the hills, a small dove climbs the side of the screen as you read, value cards stack on top of each other, and services reveal photos on hover
    Best for
    Younger participants and recruiting support workers
    Open Rise

What we learned from other disability support sites

  • Award-winning sites such as Aruma (2026 Communicator Awards) and My Supports (2026 NYX gold) use separate paths for participants, carers, referrers and staff. Concept 2 does this directly, and all three have referral and careers routes.
  • Trust signals do most of the persuading: NDIS registration, real photos, testimonials and quick response times. Every concept puts these on the homepage.
  • Accessibility is expected. The NDIS's own site targets WCAG 2.2 AA. All three concepts include skip links, keyboard support, visible focus, readable contrast and a reduced-motion mode that switches the animation off for visitors who need it.
  • The best sites use plain language and a clear phone number. Each concept keeps "Call" one tap away.
